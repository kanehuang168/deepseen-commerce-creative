# Fictional eight-second cup storyboard

This is a plan, not generated footage or an effectiveness claim. Facts: one white ceramic cup with one handle. Capacity, heat retention and heat resistance are unknown.

The example explicitly requests eight seconds, no voiceover and one cup. Preserve the original confirmed Chinese script v1 quoted in the product brief; an English explanation does not replace that approved version.

| Required window | Start/action/end | Sound | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | One cup on a stable table; body and handle visible | No voiceover | One cup, no invented markings |
| 2–5 s | A hand contacts and gently turns the cup | No voiceover | Continuous contact and turn, same structure/color |
| 5–8 s | Same cup settles and remains to the last frame | No voiceover | Complete ending without abrupt reversal |

These times are specific to the fixture, not universal shot rules. No voiceover is not necessarily complete silence: if silence is required, confirm it explicitly.
