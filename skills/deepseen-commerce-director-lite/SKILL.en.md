# DeepSeen Commerce Creative — Script and Direction

Primarily for TikTok sellers. Turn supplied product facts and goals into a script or a shootable storyboard. Reply in the user's language; preserve the exact language and wording of a confirmed script unless the user authorizes a separate revision or translation.

## Preserve the authoritative input

An existing confirmed script goes directly to direction: do not redo market research or rewrite it. With no script, draft a hook, demonstration/proof and appropriate ending from the stated objective. Do not force fixed dialogue, shot counts or exaggerated promises.

Separate facts, user requirements, creative assumptions and unknowns. Do not invent capacity, performance, sales, efficacy or reference-product attributes. A product advisor's hypothesis is not a verified selling point.

## Make action observable

For necessary shots specify corresponding script, starting state, contact/action, intermediate state, end state, assets, sound and observable acceptance. Use timing windows only when explicitly required, not mechanical equal slices. Maintain identity, count, structure, color and markings. Simplify difficult actions or request appropriate references rather than adding endless prohibitions. One successful example is not a model guarantee.

Deliver the product-fact sheet, confirmed script/version, storyboard, unknowns, production risks and acceptance checklist. The [fictional cup plan](assets/cup-plan.en.md) is an example, not a generated asset. Without footage say “Plan complete; media has not been produced.” A request for English copy creates a separately confirmed version; do not silently replace an approved Chinese script.

## Actual production and independent review

When actual footage exists, optionally hand confirmed requirements, essential product evidence and the final file to deepseen-asset-quality-audit. Do not hand over director reasoning or self-scores as quality evidence. Existing footage from other tools can go directly to the reviewer.

Production requires a genuinely available tool, live capability/quote and applicable authorization. Do not invent tool names, paid submissions, results or publication. Missing capability still allows delivery of the plan. Keep successful clips and prior versions; feedback revises the affected part rather than automatically restarting product selection. See [handoff fields](references/handoff.en.md).

## Delivery check

Check product facts, target market and confirmed script; label planned versus observed shots, language and duration; deliver the storyboard and unresolved items without claiming a finished video.

## Basic experience: deliver a usable plan

Use supplied product facts to deliver the requested angle, script draft or storyboard: product and audience, established facts, script version, shots and materials, unknowns and open questions. Preserve a confirmed script; state when no finished video exists. For cloud production, the user may check actual DeepSeen capabilities and pricing.
